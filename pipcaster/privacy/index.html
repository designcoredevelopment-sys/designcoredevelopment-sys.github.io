Pipcaster Privacy Policy

Design Core Development · Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Pipcaster (the "game") on Android, who collects it, and the choices you have. Pipcaster is made by Design Core Development ("we", "us").

The short version

  • We don't run any servers and we don't collect your personal information ourselves.
  • Your characters, saves and settings are stored only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device information to show and measure those ads.
  • Purchases (such as "Remove ads") are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your characters, progress, decks, items, settings (such as color scheme and text speed), any battle in progress, and whether you own the "Remove ads" purchase. This data stays on your device in the game's private storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and there are no accounts or logins.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Unless you have bought "Remove ads", the game shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on menu screens and after battles, and an occasional full-screen ad between battles. To show, limit, measure and prevent fraud in these ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (for example device model, operating system version and language),
  • how you interact with ads (for example views and taps).

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. You can read how Google uses information from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your ad choices and consent

  • Consent: if you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are personalized. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).
  • No ads at all: buying "Remove ads" in Settings → Ads turns off all ads in the game.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment information under its own terms and privacy policy; we never receive your card or billing details. The game only learns from Google Play whether your Google account owns the "Remove ads" purchase, so it can turn ads off and restore the purchase on a new device.

Crash and diagnostic data

Google Play may provide us with anonymous, aggregated crash and performance reports (for example "the app crashed on this device model"), according to your device's settings. These reports do not identify you.

Children

Pipcaster is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes all game data on your device.
  • Characters can also be deleted inside the game from the main menu.
  • To manage data held by Google, use your Android advertising settings above and your Google Account at myaccount.google.com.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe or privacy laws in California and other US states), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we do not collect or store personal information ourselves, most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google. You are always welcome to contact us with questions.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting different information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
